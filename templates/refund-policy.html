Refund and Cancellation Policy

How optional one-time support payments, cancellation and refund requests are handled.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

BumpMarks itself is free. This policy applies only if you choose to make an optional one-time support payment toward development, maintenance or hosting.

1. Nature of support payments

Optional support payments are voluntary. They do not purchase a physical product, medical service, clinical monitoring, subscription or guaranteed software feature.

2. Cancellation

You can abandon or close the payment flow before a payment is completed. Once a one-time support payment has been successfully completed, there is no recurring subscription to cancel.

3. Refund eligibility

Support payments are generally non-refundable. However, you may request review of a refund if:

  • the same payment was charged more than once;
  • you entered an amount in error and contacted us promptly;
  • a technical problem caused an incorrect charge; or
  • you believe a payment was made without your authorization.

4. How to request a refund

Email aryamondal723@gmail.com within 7 calendar days of the transaction. Include the transaction/reference ID, payment date, amount, and the email or phone number used for the payment.

Please do not include movement history or medical information in a refund request.

5. Review and processing

Refund requests are reviewed individually. If approved, the refund will normally be initiated to the original payment method within 5 business days after approval. The bank, card network, UPI provider or payment processor may take additional time to make the refunded amount visible.

Payment-provider charges, taxes or settlement adjustments will be handled according to applicable provider rules and law.

6. Failed or reversed payments

If a transaction fails, is automatically reversed or never reaches a successful status, a separate refund may not be necessary. Your bank or payment provider controls the timing of any automatic reversal.

7. Contact

For a payment or refund issue, email aryamondal723@gmail.com or call +91 99036 45467.

BumpMarks is a private movement logging tool. It is not a diagnostic or medical decision-making system.
