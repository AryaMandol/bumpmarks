Privacy Policy

How BumpMarks handles local movement records, website data, optional support payments and contact information.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

BumpMarks is local-first. Movement records, notes, settings and doctor instructions are stored in your browser by default and are not uploaded to a BumpMarks account or database.

1. What BumpMarks stores

The tracker stores the information you enter directly in your browser's local storage. This can include movement entries, entry times, catch-up timing labels, daily notes, tracking preferences, targets and doctor instructions.

BumpMarks does not require an account or sign-in and does not operate a cloud database containing your movement log.

2. Website and hosting data

When you load the BumpMarks website, the hosting and network providers that deliver the site may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, request time and security logs. This information is handled as part of operating and securing the website.

BumpMarks does not add advertising trackers or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs to the tracker.

3. Payments and support

If you choose to make an optional support payment, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. The provider processes the payment details needed to complete the transaction under its own privacy terms.

BumpMarks does not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN, bank password or other payment credentials. We may receive transaction information such as the payment amount, status, reference number and contact fields you provide to the payment provider.

4. Contact messages

If you contact BumpMarks by email or phone, the information you choose to provide is used to respond to your request, investigate a technical or payment issue, or handle a refund request.

Please do not send medical records, movement history or other sensitive health information unless it is genuinely necessary for the support issue.

5. Cookies and tracking

BumpMarks does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. The Progressive Web App uses a service worker and browser cache so the application can continue to work offline. Those browser features are used for application delivery, not behavioral advertising.

6. Export, backup and deletion

You can export movement information as CSV, create a JSON backup, restore a backup, or delete all locally stored BumpMarks data from within the app.

Files you export remain under your control. If you email, upload or share an exported file, the receiving service may process that file under its own terms.

7. Data retention

Local movement data remains in your browser until you delete it, clear browser/site storage, uninstall in a way that removes site data, or the browser removes it. BumpMarks cannot recover locally deleted data unless you previously created your own backup.

Support emails and payment-related correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain transaction records, comply with legal obligations or prevent abuse.

8. Security

BumpMarks uses browser security controls, HTTPS in production, restrictive response headers and a local-first architecture to reduce unnecessary data exposure. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

BumpMarks is intended for adults using it to record pregnancy-related movement information. It is not designed as a service for children to create accounts or submit personal information.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the product, hosting setup or payment flow changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Privacy questions can be sent to aryamondal723@gmail.com.

BumpMarks is a private movement logging tool. It is not a diagnostic or medical decision-making system.
